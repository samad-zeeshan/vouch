Falcon Pay Closes Series A to Expand Across the Gulf

Falcon Pay, the fastest-growing fintech in the Gulf, today announced it has closed a $13 million Series A funding round led by Gulf Ventures. The round will fund the expansion of its cross-border payments app for small businesses across the region.

Founded in 2018, Falcon Pay serves over 38,000 customers. The company is headquartered in Dubai and led by CEO Layla Haddad.

"This round lets us bring fast, fair payments to every small business in the region," said Layla Haddad, CEO of Falcon Pay.
